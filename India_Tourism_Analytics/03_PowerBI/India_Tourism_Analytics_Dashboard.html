Executive Tourism Insights Panel

1. Western & Northern Circuit Dominance: Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Delhi account for nearly 45% of all tourism revenue generated nationwide.

2. UNESCO Heritage Multiplier: UNESCO World Heritage attractions generate 3.4x higher international visitor footfall and yielding significantly higher average revenue per visitor.

3. Winter Peak Concentration: The Winter season (Nov–Feb) accounts for over 38% of total annual footfall, demonstrating high seasonality across heritage and wildlife destinations.
